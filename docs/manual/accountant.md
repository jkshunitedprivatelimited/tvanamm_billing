# Accountant guide

Review the financial records available to your account. Outlet scope is enforced by your access.

[Back to all guides](README.md)

## Review daily money

**Where:** Reports → Sales overview

Check the recorded sales and payment totals.

1. Select the outlet scope and date range.
2. Compare gross sales, discounts, refunds and net sales.
3. Review cash and UPI payments.
4. Open the relevant bill or adjustment details when investigating a difference.

Net sales is not profit. A payment record is not a substitute for checking the bank or payment-provider settlement.

## Review cash and expenses

**Where:** Reports → Cash reconciliation / Expenses

Explain drawer differences and spending.

1. Compare opening cash, cash transactions, drawer expenses and counted closing cash.
2. Review variance reasons recorded during register closure.
3. Check each expense’s employee, reason and payment source.
4. Check linked local purchases before counting them as a second expense.
5. Use only the review actions your account permits.

Owner-paid, employee-paid and UPI expenses are not cash removed from the drawer. Employee reimbursements need their own follow-up.

## Export and investigate

**Where:** Reports → Exports & records; Business overview

Prepare records for reconciliation and follow up on unusual entries.

1. Set the correct scope and dates before downloading.
2. Download the relevant CSV and retain the report period with your records.
3. Use business or activity records available to your account to investigate changes.
4. Ask JKSH AI a finance question, then verify the answer against the source report.

Your role does not provide franchise setup, menu publication or recipe-draft creation access.

## Troubleshooting and support

**Where:** The screen where the issue occurred

Resolve common issues without duplicating records.

1. If OTP delivery fails, check the mobile number and connection, then use the offered retry after the countdown. Never send an OTP to support.
2. If a report is empty, check the outlet, dates and whether offline bills have synced.
3. If a save has an unclear result, refresh or check history before submitting another entry.
4. If stock is wrong, check receipts, units, recipe links, counts and wastage before adjusting it.
5. For an unresolved issue, record the screen name, time, outlet, record reference and exact error. Share a screenshot with private information hidden.

Use only the actions available to your role. An unavailable action can mean missing setup, missing access or a temporary connection problem; it does not mean you should create duplicate records.

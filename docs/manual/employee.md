# Employee daily guide

Use this guide at the billing counter. Your entries are recorded against your employee account and outlet.

[Back to all guides](README.md)

## Start your day

**Where:** Billing → Sign in → Start shift

Identify who is working and record the cash available before sales begin.

1. Sign in on the registered billing device with your own PIN and confirm your name.
2. If the store register is closed, count the money in the drawer and enter the opening cash when prompted.
3. Start your shift. If another employee already opened the register, use the existing shared register.
4. Open Staff menu to record attendance. A colleague can identify themselves with their own PIN without changing the cashier.

Opening cash belongs to the shared register. Do not enter it again just because a second employee starts work.

## Record milk or sugar bought locally

**Where:** Billing → Stock & purchases → Local purchase

Add the supplies you bought and record their cost together.

1. Select an allowed local-purchase item, such as milk or sugar.
2. Enter the quantity and check the unit. For example, enter 5 litres for five litres of milk.
3. Enter the total amount paid, not the price of one litre or kilogram.
4. Select the actual payment source: cash drawer, outlet UPI, owner paid, or employee paid.
5. Add a purchase note, check the details, and save once.
6. Check the recent entries to confirm that the purchase was recorded.

The purchase creates stock and one linked expense. Do not add the same payment again under Other expense. Tea powder must be ordered through the supply system; it is not available for local purchase. Employee-paid entries record money owed, not proof that reimbursement has been paid.

## Make a sale

**Where:** Billing

Record items sold and how the customer paid.

1. Search or select a menu category, then tap the items the customer wants.
2. Check quantities, add-ons and the bill total. Remove any accidental selections.
3. If needed, use Add bill discount and enter the required details. Add customer details only when needed.
4. Select the actual payment method and complete the required payment fields.
5. Confirm the sale, then print or reprint the receipt as needed. Use Bill history to find the completed bill.

Billing can continue when stock has not been entered or is insufficient. A bill alone does not guarantee ingredient balances are accurate: stock deduction also needs a published, linked recipe and successful syncing. Do not confirm the same sale twice if its result is unclear; check Bill history first.

## Record another expense

**Where:** Billing → Stock & purchases → Other expense

Record spending that is not already saved as a local stock purchase.

1. Choose the expense category and enter the total amount.
2. Select the actual payment source.
3. Write a clear reason, such as delivery charge or cleaning expense.
4. Save and check the entry before continuing.

Only cash-drawer expenses reduce the expected drawer balance. Do not duplicate a milk or sugar purchase already recorded under Local purchase.

## Record wastage

**Where:** Billing → Stock & purchases → Record wastage

Explain a known loss of stock, such as spilled milk or spoiled ingredients.

1. Select the item that was lost.
2. Enter only the quantity lost and check the unit.
3. Choose the reason and add details: for example, 0.5 litres of milk spilled during preparation.
4. Review and save. Check the recent entry.

Wastage reduces stock; it does not record another purchase expense. Never enter the same loss twice. Batch-tracked goods need the batch-specific stock workspace.

## Count what is physically left

**Where:** Billing → Stock & purchases → Count stock

Compare the stock on the shelf with the recorded balance.

1. Physically count or measure the selected item.
2. Enter the quantity actually remaining, not the amount missing.
3. Add a note explaining the count and submit it.
4. Tell the owner that a count is ready to review. The balance changes after approval.

Example: if the app shows 6 litres but you have 5 litres, enter 5. If a loss was already recorded as wastage, do not deduct it again. Counts are snapshots; wastage records a particular loss.

## Finish your shift or close the store

**Where:** Billing → Finish

Keep staff attendance and shared cash accurate.

1. Review your shift expenses. Add missing expenses or confirm that all expenses are recorded or that there were none.
2. Choose Finish my work when another employee will continue billing. This ends your own shift and attendance and signs you out; the store register stays open.
3. Choose Close store only when the shared register should close. Count the actual cash and enter it.
4. If the cash differs from the expected amount, enter a clear reason and complete the close.
5. Check that your work is finished before leaving the device.

Each employee records their own attendance. Signing out or changing cashier is not the same as closing the store. Sync pending bills before switching or finishing when the app requires it.

## Handle offline billing and retries

**Where:** Billing → Connection status / recovery

Preserve sales if the internet is interrupted.

1. Use offline billing only when the device already has the required menu and offline access available.
2. Keep the same browser and device until saved bills have synced.
3. Reconnect, allow pending sales to sync, and review Bill history before retrying a sale.
4. If a stock entry reports a pending save, retry that saved request instead of creating a second entry.
5. Ask the owner for help if recovery or an unresolved error remains.

Local purchases, wastage and counts require an internet connection. Do not clear browser storage while bills are pending. Printer setup is available from the billing printer settings.

## Troubleshooting and support

**Where:** The screen where the issue occurred

Resolve common issues without duplicating records.

1. If OTP delivery fails, check the mobile number and connection, then use the offered retry after the countdown. Never send an OTP to support.
2. If a report is empty, check the outlet, dates and whether offline bills have synced.
3. If a save has an unclear result, refresh or check history before submitting another entry.
4. If stock is wrong, check receipts, units, recipe links, counts and wastage before adjusting it.
5. For an unresolved issue, record the screen name, time, outlet, record reference and exact error. Share a screenshot with private information hidden.

Use only the actions available to your role. An unavailable action can mean missing setup, missing access or a temporary connection problem; it does not mean you should create duplicate records.

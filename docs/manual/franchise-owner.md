# Franchise owner guide

Manage your own outlet: sales, team, menu, expenses and stock. Select the correct outlet whenever your account has more than one.

[Back to all guides](README.md)

## Accept your invitation and set up the outlet

**Where:** Invitation link → Setup; then Overview

Get access and provide the outlet details once.

1. Open the complete invitation link in your browser and verify the invited mobile number with the OTP.
2. Complete the requested outlet profile and submit it.
3. Check setup progress. An accepted invitation and a completed outlet activation are separate steps.
4. After activation, open your outlet and use Team & devices to set up employees and the billing device.
5. Confirm that a menu has been published before the team begins billing.

An outlet count of zero during setup does not mean your invitation failed. Check whether outlet details are still awaiting activation. Do not share employee PINs or OTPs in help requests.

## Review your outlet each day

**Where:** Outlet overview

See sales and the tasks that need attention.

1. Choose Today, Last 7 days or Last 30 days.
2. Review net sales, completed bills, average bill and low-stock items.
3. Check the sales breakdown for cash, UPI, discounts and refunds.
4. Open the relevant task or report when something needs attention.

Zero sales means no recorded sales for the selected scope and dates. It does not prove the outlet was closed. Check pending offline billing and filters if sales are missing.

## Manage employees and billing devices

**Where:** Overview → Your outlet → Billing & team → Team & devices

Maintain the people and devices allowed to work at your outlet.

1. Open the correct outlet and the Team & devices section.
2. Add or update employees using the available employee controls and assign their access.
3. Set up the billing device using the device registration flow shown on screen.
4. Review attendance in the Attendance section and investigate missing check-ins or check-outs.
5. Update or revoke access when someone leaves. Complete fresh verification if prompted.

Employees should use their own identity. Multiple employees can work with one shared store register without opening cash being counted twice.

## Change your outlet menu

**Where:** Menu → Your outlet

Manage what customers can buy at your outlet.

1. Check the selected outlet.
2. Edit the item name, category, price or other fields available for your outlet.
3. Remove an item from sale when you no longer sell it; restore it when appropriate.
4. Save your changes, then publish the outlet menu.
5. Check the billing device after it receives the new menu.

Your outlet changes do not edit other outlets or the master menu. Removing an item from sale keeps earlier bill records. Saved drafts are not yet the live billing menu.

## Find sales, discounts and refunds

**Where:** Reports → Sales overview; outlet → Bills & refunds

Understand sales and investigate adjustments.

1. Select the outlet and date range.
2. Review sales totals and payment breakdown.
3. Open the Discounts & refunds section or its shortcut in Sales overview to review the reason breakdown.
4. For a specific bill, open the outlet’s Bills & refunds section and locate the bill.
5. Use the permitted refund action with a reason when required; verify the resulting record.

Discounts reduce the selling price; refunds return money against a completed sale. Net sales is not profit. Check the original payment method and applicable refund workflow before treating a refund record as a completed external transfer.

## Use each report

**Where:** Reports

Choose a report based on the question you need answered.

1. Use Sales overview for revenue, payments, discounts and refunds.
2. Use Top-selling items for quantities and revenue of items sold at your outlet.
3. Use Team for employee sales and attendance; use Cash reconciliation to compare expected cash with counted cash.
4. Use Expenses for recorded spending and payment sources.
5. Use Stock & wastage for stock movements and known losses; use Supply orders for ordering and delivery history.
6. Use Exports & records or the visible CSV download action to export the selected data.

Keep the same date range when comparing reports. Empty records mean none were found in that period; uncounted stock is not a confirmed zero.

## Review employee spending

**Where:** Your outlet → Expenses

See who spent money, why, and how it was paid.

1. Review the employee, amount, date, payment source and reason.
2. Check linked milk or sugar purchases before approving or recording another expense.
3. Use the review actions available to approve or reverse an incorrect expense with an explanation.
4. If a linked stock purchase was wrong, review both the expense and physical stock correction.

A linked purchase cannot be reversed as stock only. Money and quantity must both be reviewed. Employee-paid spending identifies reimbursement needs; it should not be counted as cash taken from the drawer.

## Enter current stock and set alerts

**Where:** Stock & orders → Your outlet → Current stock & alerts

Start stock tracking and decide when you want a low-stock warning.

1. Choose Enter current stock when items are available.
2. Enter the measured quantity of each item and check its unit.
3. Save the opening quantities and review the resulting balances.
4. Set a minimum stock level for the items you want to monitor.
5. Review low-stock warnings and order before supplies run out.

Opening stock is optional. You can start tracking with the next confirmed delivery or local purchase. Billing continues either way. If Enter current stock is unavailable because there are no active items, the item catalogue needs setup first; do not invent quantities to enable it.

## Order and receive supplies

**Where:** Stock & orders → Order stock / Orders & deliveries / Receive deliveries

Order company supplies and add the quantity that actually arrives.

1. Choose the required items and quantities, then review prices and charges.
2. Place the order and complete payment through the available payment flow.
3. Check the order status in Orders & deliveries.
4. When supplies arrive, compare them with the delivery and record the received quantities.
5. Report shortages or damaged goods using the discrepancy controls.
6. Confirm receipt and check the stock balance.

Placing or paying for an order does not add usable stock. Confirmed receipt adds it. Tea powder is company-supplied; employees cannot add it as a local purchase. If online payment is unavailable, keep the saved order and resolve payment setup before retrying.

## Review counts, wastage and returns

**Where:** Stock & orders → Counts & wastage / Return stock

Keep stock balances aligned with what is physically available.

1. Ask the working employee to record daily milk purchases and any known wastage from Billing → Stock & purchases.
2. When checking stock, have the employee submit the quantity actually remaining.
3. Open Counts & wastage to review the submitted count, employee details and explanation.
4. Approve an eligible count after checking the difference. A reviewer cannot approve their own count.
5. Use Return stock for supplies being returned and follow the available return process.

Wastage records a known loss once. A count measures what remains. Do not deduct a previously recorded loss a second time. Automatic sales usage requires a linked, published recipe and successful sync.

## Ask JKSH AI

**Where:** JKSH AI or Ask JKSH AI about these reports

Ask questions about your own outlet data.

1. Choose the outlet and relevant dates when offered.
2. Ask a specific question, such as Which items sold most this week? or Which expenses increased?
3. Check the answer against the linked report or displayed figures.
4. Open the relevant feature yourself to make a business change.

Owner AI is limited to your permitted outlet data. It does not publish recipes, change stock or grant access. If AI is unavailable, continue using the normal reports.

## Troubleshooting and support

**Where:** The screen where the issue occurred

Resolve common issues without duplicating records.

1. If OTP delivery fails, check the mobile number and connection, then use the offered retry after the countdown. Never send an OTP to support.
2. If a report is empty, check the outlet, dates and whether offline bills have synced.
3. If a save has an unclear result, refresh or check history before submitting another entry.
4. If stock is wrong, check receipts, units, recipe links, counts and wastage before adjusting it.
5. For an unresolved issue, record the screen name, time, outlet, record reference and exact error. Share a screenshot with private information hidden.

Use only the actions available to your role. An unavailable action can mean missing setup, missing access or a temporary connection problem; it does not mean you should create duplicate records.
